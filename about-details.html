OUR JOURNEY & FOUNDATION

Hospital Introduction & History

Sulakshaa Multi Speciality Hospital was founded with a clear, noble vision: to make comprehensive, high-quality multi-speciality medical care readily accessible, compassionate, and transparent for families across Mysuru and neighboring districts of Karnataka.

Guided by senior physician Dr. Laxme Gowda, our hospital combines seasoned clinical leadership with purpose-built clinical infrastructure. Over the years, we have grown from an ambitious healthcare vision into a trusted healthcare destination, delivering round-the-clock emergency medical response, specialized consultations, modern surgical care, and intensive patient monitoring.

Every department at Sulakshaa Hospital operates on the principle of empathy, dignity, and clinical excellence — ensuring patients receive uncompromised diagnostic accuracy and personalized treatment plans in a supportive, reassuring healing environment.

OUR GUIDING PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be recognized as the most trusted and reliable multi-speciality healthcare institution in Mysuru, celebrated for exemplary clinical outcomes, empathetic patient relationships, ethical medical practice, and advanced technological capabilities accessible to all segments of society.

Our Mission

To deliver compassionate, comprehensive, and evidence-based clinical services 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We aim to continually upgrade medical infrastructure, foster patient-first clinical protocols, minimize waiting times, and empower families with health education and preventive wellness.
