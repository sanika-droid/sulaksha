About Us

A Sanctuary of Healing & Clinical Care

SULAKSHAA HOSPITAL — A Sanctuary of Healing & Clinical Care, is a premier multi-speciality hospital located in Mysuru city in the southern state of Karnataka, India. The hospital is envisioned with the aim of bringing the highest standards of holistic medical care at an affordable cost.

Healthcare at SULAKSHAA HOSPITAL is not just about treating ailments, but caring for patients with Compassion and Empathy, along with an indomitable team of Specialists and Experts, whose proficiency is backed by cutting edge equipment and modern clinical amenities to provide unmatched service to patients.

Quality Council of India (QCI) Standards

National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) Guidelines

Sulakshaa Multi Speciality Hospital adheres to stringent quality benchmarks — a mark of excellence in healthcare quality and patient safety.

Adherence to rigorous clinical benchmarks reflects our commitment to delivering the highest standards of medical care, ensuring that every patient receives safe, ethical, and evidence-based treatment. Our hospital meets stringent quality benchmarks across all areas, including patient rights, infection control, modern infrastructure, clinical staff training, and continuous quality improvement.

This recognition reinforces our mission to provide compassionate, affordable, and trusted healthcare services, guided by transparency and trust. At Sulakshaa Hospital, quality is not just a protocol — it's a promise.

Our Vision

  • To provide access to superior quality integrated health care at an affordable cost with empathy & compassion.
  • Complete care with concern.
  • To evolve as a trusted "Sanctuary of Healing" for Mysuru and surrounding regions.

Our Mission

  • Best clinical outcomes in the most effective, efficient and caring environment.
  • Continuous upgradation of advanced clinical equipment and diagnostic facilities.
  • To produce & train highly skilled health science & allied healthcare professionals.
  • Continuous community health education, preventive wellness, and ethical care.
  • To deliver accessible, affordable multi-speciality care with 24/7 emergency response.

Ethics & Transparency
